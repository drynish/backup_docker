Our Courses

Whether you're a learner driver needing extra practice, a newcomer to Quebec, or an experienced driver wanting to refresh your skills, I offer personalized coaching tailored to your needs and goals.

Learner Driver

Stressed about your exam? Failed multiple times? Haven't driven in a while and need a refresher? You've come to the right place! As a former SAAQ examiner, I've administered thousands of driving tests. I understand exactly what you're going through! Text me at 514-867-2886 and I'll explain precisely what the SAAQ examiner is looking for.

Mandatory Re-evaluation

You've received a letter from SAAQ requiring you to pass a road test or your license will be suspended. You're worried about passing because driving rules and techniques have evolved significantly. As a former SAAQ examiner with experience evaluating hundreds of people in your situation, I can help! Text me at 514-867-2886. A refresher course will be invaluable before your test, and I'll ensure you meet all success criteria.

License Exchange

You've relocated to Quebec with a foreign driver's license and need a Quebec license, but you realize the rules and techniques differ from your home country. As a former SAAQ examiner who has tested thousands of newcomers, I know exactly which driving techniques you need to adapt. Text me at 514-867-2886 and I'll help you master Quebec's road safety code and techniques.

Experienced Driver

You've been driving for years and suspect you may have picked up bad habits. A skills refresh might be just what you need! Text me at 514-867-2886 and I'll help you update your knowledge of traffic safety regulations and driving techniques.
